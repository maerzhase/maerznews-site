Technology · AI · Markets

Anthropic prices the biggest share sale ever: $83 a share, about $80 billion raised and a value near $1.5 trillion as the maker of Claude lists on Nasdaq today

The price came in above the $70 to $78 range the company had set, after investors asked for six times as many shares as were on offer. The debut is the first chance for the public to own a company built only around AI, and a test of nerves for an industry that has had a bad month.

BBC · tagesschau · heise online · Reuters · as read at 07:00 on 19 November 2026

Anthropic said on Wednesday night that it will sell more than 956 million new shares at $83 each, against a range of $70 to $78 announced earlier this month. That comes to $79.4 billion before fees, and the banks can buy up to 147 million more within 30 days, worth another $12.2 billion. A further $26 billion has been placed privately with large investors, so the total could pass $100 billion, well beyond the $75 billion SpaceX raised in June. At the issue price the company is worth about $1.5 trillion, enough to put it among the ten most valuable in the US. Trading opens later today in New York, at 15:30 Berlin time at the earliest.

Demand was not the problem. Institutional orders came to about $400 billion when the book closed on Tuesday, CNBC reports, and private investors reportedly asked for almost $100 billion more, so most will get far fewer shares than they wanted. Anthropic, founded in 2021 by Dario and Daniela Amodei and other former OpenAI staff, made $17 billion in revenue in the third quarter; analysts expect $52 billion for 2026. Its prospectus spends almost 100 pages on risks, and its spending is several times its income: it has committed close to $900 billion to chips and computing by 2045.

Never let your company be worth a single dollar more than it's worth. Dario Amodei, to JPMorgan's Jamie Dimon last week

The timing is awkward. Nvidia is 30 per cent off its peak after Jensen Huang's exit, the Federal Reserve has flagged "complex and opaque" AI debt, and about a quarter of Anthropic's revenue comes from a handful of customers, some of them also its investors and suppliers. Why it matters: this is the first public price for a pure AI developer, and it arrives with OpenAI planning to follow next year. Ovar · Housing

Finished in June, opened with ceremony in July, still empty in November: 52 families wait for their keys on Avenida D. Maria II

The two once-abandoned buildings in central Ovar were bought and rebuilt as 52 affordable flats, 11 one-bedroom, 30 two-bedroom and 11 three-bedroom, for €6.87 million from the EU recovery fund's 1.º Direito housing programme. Work ended on 29 June and the block was inaugurated on 25 July, Municipality Day. But a financial dispute between the câmara and the builder, Embeiral, which won the contract in 2024 for about €5.65 million plus VAT and is now asking for more, means the certificates needed to connect water, electricity and gas are being withheld.

Mayor Domingos Silva told the council the sums are "significant" and that he hopes to settle without going to court; he gave no date for the keys. Families told the meeting they are staying with friends and relatives, or in rentals whose contracts have already run out. The câmara has moved the start of the subsidised rents from 1 November to 1 January 2027, and admits the October cyberattack has also knocked out the phone and email lines the families would use to ask.

Federal · Energy prices

After eight hours in the Chancellery, the coalition agrees: electricity tax falls from 2.05 to 0.1 cents for everyone from January, for one year

The decision ends months of quarrelling over a promise Friedrich Merz had made his own. From 1 January 2027 households and all businesses, including the hairdressers and supermarkets left out of last year's cut for industry and farming, pay only the EU minimum. Verivox reckons a three-person household using 4,000 kWh a year saves about €93, or 7 per cent; a couple using 2,800 kWh about €65; a single person on 1,500 kWh about €35. A cap on fuel prices, replacing the expiring Tankrabatt, was agreed at the same time.

The €5.4 billion bill is to be paid from surplus money in the renewable-energy (EEG) account, which only works if power prices stay high next year. If it falls short the coalition has committed to a package of savings, with cuts to subsidies or a higher tax on capital gains named as options. Extending the cut beyond 2027 depends on finding the money in the 2028 budget.

Security · nginx

Two HTTP/2 bugs let attackers run code on nginx without logging in, one of them dating back to 2015

F5 shipped fixes on Wednesday for CVE-2026-123633 and CVE-2026-123007, both rated 9.2 and found independently by Wiz, Rapid7 and three other teams. The first is a use-after-free in reverse-proxy handling when connection slots run low; the second, a flow-control accounting error that causes an invalid memory copy, reaches back to nginx 1.9.5. Wiz showed code execution on a server that only serves static files over HTTPS, and says ASLR does not help because both bugs leak memory first.

nginx runs more than 225 million sites. Both bugs need HTTP/2 switched on; if you cannot upgrade now, Wiz advises http2 off; and removing http2 from listen. WordPress has patched dozens of bugs this week, and PHP fixes are due today.
