Technology · Chips · Business

Nvidia books $115.8 billion in a single quarter, double a year ago, and its board says Jensen Huang is not coming back

The first results without its founder beat the company's own forecast by almost $8 billion and promise $130 billion next quarter. On the same call, the new board chair shut the door Huang had tried to reopen that morning.

The Verge · heise online · BBC · Reuters · tagesschau · as read at 07:00 on 18 November 2026

Revenue reached $115.8 billion in the three months to October, up 103 per cent on a year earlier, 20 per cent on the previous quarter and well past the $108 billion Nvidia itself had pencilled in. Almost all of it, $107.6 billion, came from data centres, up 110 per cent; the edge business, which takes in gaming PCs and the new RTX Spark laptops, added $8.2 billion. Operating profit rose to $77.5 billion and net profit to a record $65.3 billion. Interim chief executive Colette Kress, who stays on as finance chief, said the ramp-up of the new Vera Rubin systems had been the fastest in the company's history, and guided to $130 billion for the current quarter. The battered shares rose about 5 per cent after hours.

The figures could only partly change the subject. Huang quit last week as chief executive, president and board member after reports of a second extramarital affair, this one inside the company; an earlier relationship with Alice Huang of Megaspeed, the Singapore firm US investigators suspect of illegally shipping Nvidia chips to China, has raised questions about sanctions. On Tuesday morning he wrote on X that he "did not live up to the standards expected of everyone" and would return if the board judged it in the interest of shareholders, staff "and America".

The Nvidia board unanimously accepted his resignation and does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO. Harvey C. Jones, Nvidia's first board chair

Jones opened the earnings call with that line and took no questions on the affair or the investigation, promising a fast search for a successor. Semafor reports that a faction on the board is still pushing to bring Huang back. The board has set up a committee to oversee national security, export controls and trade risk, reporting to it and to Kress, and Reuters says Huang is due before Congress in December. Why it matters: the AI spending boom is still accelerating, and the company that sells most of its picks and shovels now has to find a new leader while Washington looks closely at its China ties.

Espinho · Animation · 50 editions

Fifty editions of Cinanima: the oldest animation festival in Portugal, just up the coast in Espinho, comes of age in a record year for Portuguese animated features

The Cine Clube de Avanca marked the milestone with a reminder of what the Espinho festival has meant for the district. Portugal's first animated feature, "Até ao Tecto do Mundo" by Vítor Lopes, António Costa Valente and Carlos Silva, had its premiere on Cinanima's opening night in 2006, the first project the film institute ICA backed under its new support for animated features. In the twenty years that followed, only two more Portuguese animated features were made, both shown at the festival.

2026 doubles that pace: two new frame-by-frame features bring the total to five. One is "Viagens – Entre Estrelas e Ondas" by Cláudio Jordão, produced again by the Avanca club with Filmógrafo and Koto Studios and due to premiere at Tribeca Lisboa; the other is "Viana: A Lenda dos Corações de Ouro". Both, the club notes, were financed in new ways, and both the festival and the first feature were born outside the big cities, in the district of Aveiro.

Berlin · S-Bahn

The Ringbahn will be cut between Frankfurter Allee and Ostkreuz for 14 months from 2030, to replace a 1910 bridge

Planning documents published on the federal consultation portal show that the four-track bridge over Gürtelstraße in Friedrichshain is so damaged that "availability can no longer be guaranteed" and must be rebuilt. The closure is expected to start in the third quarter of 2030 and hit the S41, S42, S8 and S85; trains should run again in the third quarter of 2031.

Deutsche Bahn will use the shutdown to replace two more bridges nearby, over Wartenbergstraße and Tasdorfer Straße, which are 17 years younger but have also reached the end of their lives. Berlin is already learning to live with full closures over failing bridges; this one takes out a section of the ring itself.

Security · Java

Java4Shell: 64 letter As and an equals sign can take over a Java server through a bug in OpenJDK's Base64 decoder

Calif disclosed CVE-2026-114938 on Tuesday, scored 9.2 of 10. The flaw sits in hand-written assembly that HotSpot uses to decode Base64 faster with AVX-512 instructions on x86-64 chips: a stray "=" after 64 characters yields a negative length and a 64-byte write that runs 62 bytes past the end of the array. That lets an attacker leak memory, Heartbleed-style, or run code.

Calif showed remote code execution in default setups of widely used Java products and in sample Struts and Spring Boot apps, wherever logins or uploads decode Base64; a malicious TLS server can also hit clients. Only some processors are affected. It took one researcher, prompting OpenAI's Codex, an hour to find and two to exploit, for under $200 in tokens. Oracle's patch day carries eleven Java RCE fixes; patch now.
