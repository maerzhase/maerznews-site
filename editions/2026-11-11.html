Portugal · Weather · Storm Charles

Red from Leiria to Faro: Charles turns south overnight, the mainland is on alert, and schools, offices and supermarkets stay shut

IPMA raised nine districts to red for wind or rain, with gusts of 120 km/h and up to 140 in exposed places. The government declared a state of alert for all of Wednesday after 1,240 storm calls on Tuesday alone. Aveiro is spared the worst: orange rain, yellow wind.

RTP · Observador · OvarNews · Notícias de Aveiro · IPMA warnings as read at 06:15 on 11 November 2026

Charles changed course in the night. IPMA said late on Tuesday that the storm had shifted its track further south than forecast, and by the early hours it had put Lisboa, Setúbal, Santarém, Leiria, Portalegre, Beja and Faro on red for both wind and rain, Évora on red for wind and Castelo Branco on red for rain. On the red map the warnings speak of gusts up to 120 km/h, reaching 140 in exposed places, from late morning to 9 pm, and of waves of five to seven metres off the south and west coasts, with maximums of 13.

Mainland Portugal has been under a state of alert since midnight and for the full 24 hours. Public and private schools are closed, as are non-essential public services, museums and monuments, and the government has asked anyone who can to work from home. Pingo Doce has shut every mainland store for the day; outdoor sport is postponed and indoor games are played behind closed doors. Lisbon was closing its road tunnels, parks and museums, CP cancelled trains between Lisbon, Faro and Évora for the worst hours, and the Web Summit closed for the day.

"Difficult hours" are ahead; do not underestimate the bad weather. Prime minister Luís Montenegro, Tuesday evening

The storm had already struck Madeira overnight, where civil protection logged more than 50 incidents and the mountains were on red for gusts of 140 km/h. Up north the tone is different: Aveiro district carries an orange warning for persistent, at times heavy rain from 3 am to 9 pm and a yellow for gusts up to 90 km/h from noon. Aveiro's council has shut its markets and front desks and set up a coordination centre; the University of Aveiro and the University of Porto have suspended in-person teaching, and eleven of the district's councils are on the environment agency's list of 101 at risk of urban flooding.

"Batas & Betão": the head of the region's health authority is arrested on corruption charges and sacked the same day

Ricardo Matos, chairman of the Unidade Local de Saúde da Região de Aveiro, was detained by the Polícia Judiciária on Monday with two friends. Germany · Berlin & Brandenburg

Federal · Defence

16,869 conscientious objectors in ten months: refusals have nearly tripled since the summer

Since the military service modernisation law took effect in January, applications to refuse military service have climbed steeply, according to the federal office that handles them. Almost 1,900 arrived in October alone. By the end of June the count stood at 5,862; by 30 September it was 14,977; by 31 October, 16,869. For comparison, the whole of 2025 brought 3,879 and 2024 just 2,249.

Conscription has been suspended since 2011 and service remains voluntary, but the medical screening is now compulsory for every man from 18, and the Basic Law still lets anyone refuse on grounds of conscience in advance. Experts point to fear of the Russian threat; reports of sexual misconduct in the Bundeswehr, the subject of a study being presented today, are also thought to play a part.

Porto gives up its Christmas ice rink so the housing camp can stay in Praça D. João I

Security · AI coding

Grok Build's "private" share links leave 35,000 vibe-coded apps open to anyone, SpaceXSI's own included

Grok Build, the vibe-coding agent from Elon Musk's SpaceXSI, lets users share apps through randomised URLs meant to be known only to those who get the link. A design decision by its developers makes it possible to enumerate thousands of live projects anyway, and except in rare cases opening one reveals all the app's data.

Security firm Claroc counted 35,000 exposed apps holding internal business plans, ad proposals, medical exam results, a celebrity cruise line's galley checklists, and employee names and ID numbers, some of it belonging to SpaceXSI itself. The lesson for everyone shipping share links: an unguessable URL is not access control if the list of URLs can be walked.

Spain · Housing

Spain stops work today over rents, its first big general strike since 2012, called by tenants

The idea came not from the unions but from the tenants' unions founded in Madrid and Barcelona in 2017, which also ran the protest camps after the eviction of the late pensioner Maricarmen. UGT and Comisiones Obreras have joined in, demanding higher wages, nationwide rent cuts and tighter rules on property companies and investment funds.

Rents have climbed steeply since 2015; a small flat in Barcelona now costs about €1,200, the national minimum wage. It is the same fight as the acampadas in Lisbon and Porto, and it will shape Spain's coming election campaign.
