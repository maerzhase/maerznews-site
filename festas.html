Festas em Honra de São Miguel

romaria · Válega · Ovar · held 29 Sep – 4 Oct 2026

For São Miguel. Usually end of September into early October, around São Miguel's day (29 September).

Programme & sources

A romaria at the Capela de São Miguel, one of the oldest chapels in the municipality (documented in 1002), revived by a committee of five young locals after a hiatus.

2026: 29 Sep: 21-shot salute, 20h solemn mass. 2 Oct: Grupo Trovão, fireworks. 3 Oct: Bombos de N. Sra. do Desterro, Banda MP3, fireworks. 4 Oct: bands, 15h30 solemn mass and procession, Banda Tita do Agreste, fireworks.

Festas em Honra de Nossa Senhora d'Ajuda

festa · Espinho · held 7–21 Sep 2026

For Nossa Senhora da Ajuda. Usually two weeks in September, main procession on the third Sunday.

Programme & sources

The festa of Espinho's patron, protector of the sea trades, organised by the Município de Espinho: a candle procession, a flower carpet, a Majestosa Procissão with blessing of the sea, big concerts and fireworks at Praia da Baía.

2026: 13 Sep: romaria with folk groups. 16 Sep: candle procession. 17 Sep: Sinfónica 4500 with Paulo de Carvalho. 19 Sep: flower carpet, Rui Veloso, piromusical fireworks. 20 Sep: solemn mass, 16h30 Majestosa Procissão e Bênção do Mar, Vizinhos, fireworks. 21 Sep: re-creation of the Feira das Cebolas.

Festas em Honra de Nossa Senhora da Nazaré (Festas do Mar de Cortegaça)

romaria · Praia de Cortegaça · Ovar · held 4–7 Sep 2026

For Nossa Senhora da Nazaré. Usually first weekend of September (Infopédia: first Sunday of September).

Programme & sources

Held for over 140 years at the beach chapel of Nossa Senhora da Nazaré. In 2025 OvarNews called it the biggest romaria in the region: a morning procession of more than twenty andores runs from the Souto to the beach, and an afternoon procession ends with the Bênção do Mar.

2026: Fri 4: Bruno Cordeiro, DJ H'Alma. Sat 5: DNA, midnight fireworks, DJ Sandro Amaral. Sun 6: 9h procession from Largo do Souto to the beach with Banda Boa União and the Esmoriz firefighters' fanfarra; solemn mass at the chapel; 16h procession with blessing of the sea; Nuno Ribeiro at 22h; piromusical fireworks; DJ Renato C. Mon 7: NovaSom Band, closing fireworks. Organised by the Comissão de Festas de Cortegaça.

Festas do Mar do Furadouro (Senhora e Senhor da Piedade)

romaria · Furadouro · Ovar · held 4–14 Sep 2026

For Nossa Senhora da Piedade and Senhor da Piedade. Usually early to mid-September, main weekend around the second weekend of September.

Programme & sources

The fishing community's sea festival. On Friday night two candle processions leave the Capela dos Campos (Ovar) and the Furadouro chapel and meet at Carregal. On Sunday a solemn procession gives the blessing of the sea from the central breakwater, over a traditional flower carpet.

2026: Sat 5: lighting ceremony, Swaly and DJ Dias. Fri 11: candle processions, Banda Tieta do Agreste. Sat 12: Miguel Ramiro, Bruno Azevedo, Banda GPS, midnight fireworks at the Paredão Marítimo Central. Sun 13: 11h field mass, 15h30 Majestosa Procissão with bênção do mar (Fanfarra BV Ovar, Banda Boa União), Beto Rocha, Banda Time. Mon 14: Grupo Sigma, closing morteiros. Organised by the Comissão Amigos do Furadouro.

Romaria de São Paio da Torreira

romaria · Torreira · Murtosa · held 4–8 Sep 2026

For São Paio. Usually early September, ending on 8 September.

Programme & sources

The best-known lagoon festa of the Ria de Aveiro and one of Portugal's biggest romarias: rusgas, chinchorro and bateira sailing races, the moliceiro regatta and panel contest, fireworks over the sea and over the ria, and a closing procession.

2026: Chinchorro race; moliceiro regatta and panel contest; bateira sailing regatta; rusgas contest. Fogo do Mar (night of 5/6) and Fogo da Ria (night of 7/8). Revenge of the 90's, Edmundo Inácio, Marisa Liz, David Antunes, Toy. 8 Sep: field mass and majestic procession.

Festas em Honra de Nossa Senhora do Amparo

festa · Válega · Ovar · held until 17 Aug 2026

For Nossa Senhora do Amparo. Usually mid-August, around 15 August.

Programme & sources

Válega's patronal festa. Thousands follow the procession through the town centre over carpets of coloured salt made by the valeguenses; there is an ethnographic parade and a mass for emigrants.

2026: Sat 15 Aug: 16h solemn mass, then procession with Banda de São João de Loure and Fanfarra de Lourosa over salt carpets; Bruna and Nuno Portugal; midnight fireworks. Sun 16: mass for emigrants, 17h cortejo etnográfico, Os Solitários. Mon 17: Irmãos Leais, closing fireworks.

Romaria em Honra de Nossa Senhora do Desterro

romaria · Arada · Ovar · held until 13 Apr 2026

For Nossa Senhora do Desterro. Usually April, over a long weekend.

Programme & sources

A romaria at the Capela da Senhora do Desterro (1663), which local legend says stands on the rock where the Virgin appeared. It draws many thousands of pilgrims and is run by the Irmandade de Nossa Senhora do Desterro.

2026: Sat 11 Apr: Grupo Trovão, singer Kássio, fireworks. Processions on Sunday 12 Apr (11h) and Monday 13 Apr (10h30). The 2026 edition put the stress on the religious side, with no big-name acts.

Other years: 25–28 Apr 2025

Jan20Tue

Festa das Fogaceiras

festa · Santa Maria da Feira · held 20 Jan 2026

For São Sebastião. Usually 20 January (São Sebastião's day), with a programme all January.

Programme & sources

A vow to São Sebastião kept for over five centuries: about 250 girls in white carry fogaças (sweet bread) on their heads from the Câmara to the Igreja Matriz, a solemn mass blesses them, and a procession goes through the historic centre.

2026: 10h30 civic cortege, 11h solemn mass with blessing of the fogaças, 15h30 Procissão das Fogaceiras. The month-long programme ended on 31 Jan with Fernando Daniel and the four municipal bands at Europarque.
